## v1.0.4

Pinned apps that show at every icon size, and a terminal you can tap right up to its edges.

### Changes

- Changing page, and the swipes that open the keyboard and the status bar, now start only on the border around the terminal and the space just outside it. Taps, long presses and mouse clicks reach the terminal all the way to its edges.

### Fixes

- Pinned apps and the app drawer no longer disappear with the Docked style at the smallest icon size (#49).

### Editions

Shipped as `nix-v1.0.4` and `vaj-v1.0.4`. Nothing was exclusive to an edition.
