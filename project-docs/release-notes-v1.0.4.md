## v1.0.4

Pinned apps that show at every icon size, and text selection on the terminal's first lines.

### Changes

- To change page from the top of the terminal, hold right on its border or the space above it, then drag sideways.

### Fixes

- Pinned apps and the app drawer no longer disappear with the Docked style at the smallest icon size (#49).
- Long-pressing the terminal's first lines selects text instead of changing page.

### Editions

Shipped as `nix-v1.0.4` and `vaj-v1.0.4`. Nothing was exclusive to an edition.
